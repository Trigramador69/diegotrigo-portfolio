## Problem

A résumé says what you can do, but rarely how you work: communication, leadership, teamwork. And what does show up is hard to verify.

## My role

Full stack and AI developer on a team of three. We built HireTree in **72 hours** at **ETH Bolivia Buildathon 2025**, where we won **1st place in the AI & Web3 track**.

## Solution

A decentralized platform that connects talent with verified job opportunities:

- **Soft-skill assessment with LLMs**, to measure what a traditional résumé doesn't show.
- **Verifiable on-chain credentials (CV-NFT)** that any recruiter can check, deployed on **Arbitrum** and **Avalanche**.

## Stack

Next.js and TypeScript for the app, LLMs for assessment and Solidity for the smart contracts.

## Outcome

1st place in the AI & Web3 track at ETH Bolivia Buildathon 2025. The winner certificate was issued on-chain as an NFT by Ethereum Bolivia and Unlock Protocol DAO.
